Connection Setup

Instructions:

  1. One person clicks "Create Offer", copies their signal and sends it to the other person
  2. The other person pastes the signal, clicks "Create Answer", then copies and sends their signal back
  3. The first person pastes the answer signal and clicks "Connect"
  4. Connection established! Audio starts automatically, camera is off by default

Note: When screen sharing, your local video will minimize to prevent mirror loop effects.
